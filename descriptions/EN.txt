[b]Big fun that fits in your pocket[/b]
Built on the Handheld Game Console you already know, the SpiffBoy finally does what it promised: hours of adventure in the palm of your hand and music you will be humming for days. Every cartridge is a new surprise. Find them, slide them in, and see for yourself.

[b]Just like the real thing[/b]
The SpiffBoy was built to behave like the handheld consoles of the era, down to the little details. If you would have tried it on yours back then, go ahead and try it on the SpiffBoy. You might be surprised.

[b]Designed for the way you live[/b]
- Built-in headphone jack, so you can stay focused on your game without drawing attention from the people around you.
- A sound that carries: play it loud, and you may draw a crowd you did not invite.
- Runs on batteries, so you can play anywhere, even when the power is out.
- Play with the keyboard or a controller.
- Nothing beats a good game to chase the boredom away and lift your spirits.


[img]https://ohhoop.github.io/SpiffBoy/i/cartridges.png[/img]
[b]Out there, waiting for you[/b]
Cartridges and link cables are hiding all over Knox County, in the places you would expect a kid to leave them. Some lucky consoles are even found with a game still inside. The hunt is part of the fun.

[b]Your progress travels with you[/b]
Every cartridge keeps its own save. Lend it to a friend, trade it, or keep it in your pocket: your progress goes wherever the cartridge goes.


[img]https://ohhoop.github.io/SpiffBoy/i/spifflink.png[/img]
Plug the SpiffBoy Link Cable between two consoles and challenge a friend, as long as they stay close. Online or side by side in split screen, your friend only has to accept the cable and the game starts in their hands.


[img]https://ohhoop.github.io/SpiffBoy/i/yours.png[/img]
Tune the SpiffBoy to your liking: the sandbox options decide how it fits in your world, from the crowd its sound draws to the good mood it brings, the controls settings let you pick your buttons, and the mod options shape how it looks on your screen.

[b]Make your own games[/b]
Always dreamed of making your own game? Any mod can add its own cartridges to the SpiffBoy. The [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3825220785]SpiffBoy SDK[/url] has everything you need to build one: a guide, two example cartridges to learn from, a blank cartridge for your label, and tools to make and test your own game. The console is built to mimic a real physical console, with its technical constraints.


[img]https://ohhoop.github.io/SpiffBoy/i/qa.png[/img]
Q: Does it work in multiplayer?
A: Yes.

Q: Does every player need it on a server?
A: Only the server needs it in its mod and Workshop lists. Players download it automatically when they join.

Q: Is it compatible with other mods?
A: Yes, as long as they do not change the vanilla Handheld Game Console.

Q: Does it work with Project Viewpoint and pz3d?
A: Yes. If anything looks off, please report it in the Bug Reports discussion.

Q: Can I keep the console without its effects on gameplay?
A: Yes, the sandbox options can turn them off.

Q: My console will not turn on.
A: It needs batteries.

Q: Is it translated?
A: Into every language the game supports.

Q: Will there be more games?
A: Other mods can add them through the SpiffBoy SDK.

Q: Can you make Pokem...?
A: Certainly not, I don't want to be sued into oblivion.

Q: Is it safe to add or remove mid-save?
A: Yes. You can also remove it safely, but of course you will lose your cartridge save files.
